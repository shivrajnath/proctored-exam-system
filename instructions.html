Before you begin

  • Make sure your camera and microphone are working.
  • Use a stable internet connection and keep your device connected to power if possible.
  • Stay in a well-lit place and keep your face clearly visible to the camera.
  • Do not leave the exam tab or switch to other applications during the exam.

Proctoring rules

  • Camera and microphone access are required for the proctored exam.
  • The system may detect multiple people, mobile phones, unusual gaze, tab switching, and other restricted activity.
  • Proctoring events are monitored during the exam. This prototype does not upload media recordings.
  • Follow the exam rules and keep only the required materials with you.
Important: Once the exam starts, the timer will begin. Make sure you are ready before continuing.
